Purpose and use of the app

AutoMetronom is a metronome that listens to music through the microphone, estimates tempo, time signature and downbeat, and locks a click onto it. You may use the app, within applicable law, for your own non-commercial playing and practice.

Accuracy

Detection is an automatic estimate and may be off with live music, changing tempo, noise or unusual rhythms. Do not rely on the app alone for performances.

Third-party services

For song recognition and tempo lookup the app uses services from Apple (Shazam/ShazamKit), Deezer and GetSongBPM. Their availability and terms are outside our control. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
