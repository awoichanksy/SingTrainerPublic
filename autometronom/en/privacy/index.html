In short: AutoMetronom has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Live microphone audio is analysed on your device and is never recorded. Only the lookups described below leave your device. This policy applies in all regions; see the section for your location.

Data handling

AutoMetronom processes the following information to provide its features:

  • Microphone: Audio is analysed on your device to detect tempo, time signature and downbeat. Nothing is recorded or stored, and live audio is not sent to us.
  • Song recognition: To recognise the playing song, a short audio fingerprint (a signature, not a recording) is sent to Shazam (Apple) via ShazamKit.
  • Tempo lookup: After a song is recognised, its title, artist and, where available, its ISRC code are sent to Deezer and GetSongBPM to look up the tempo. No personal identifiers are sent.
  • Settings and song values: Your settings and the values you save per song are stored only on your device.

Storage and deletion

All data stays on your device. You can reset saved song values in the app, and deleting the app removes all local data. AutoMetronom does not use iCloud and runs no server of its own. Separate device backups managed by you or Apple may retain copies.

Services

AutoMetronom is free and contains no purchases or subscriptions. Song recognition is provided by Apple (ShazamKit); tempo data is looked up at Deezer and GetSongBPM, which process technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. Support emails are used only to respond to your request. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technically necessary access data such as IP address, browser information, and access time.

Regional information

Switzerland

Under applicable Swiss data protection law, you may request access, correction, or deletion. Contact us at the address above. We do not collect personal data ourselves; requests concerning lookups concern the third parties named above.

European Union and EEA

Where the GDPR applies, processing on your device is needed to provide the features you request. Song recognition and tempo lookup take place at your request and rely on performance of a contract and, for the lookup, legitimate interests in a working app. You may exercise your GDPR rights and contact a data protection authority.

United States

We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for advertising or tracking. Residents of certain US states may request access or deletion by emailing the address above.

Mainland China

AutoMetronom is designed to work without an account. The lookups described above are sent to third-party services that may be located outside mainland China. Use of these functions is optional at your request; contact us for any privacy question.

Other regions

We do not collect personal data ourselves. Please contact us at the address above for any privacy request; local rights depend on the laws applicable to you.

Your rights

You manage your data directly in the app. For privacy requests, email support@canusing.com.
